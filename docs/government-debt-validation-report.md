## 2. Coverage

- SBP quarterly stocks (8 series, billion PKR): 67 quarter-ends, 2009-06-30 to 2026-09-30; every series covers the same dates. Quarters SBP does not publish in this dataset: 2009-09-30, 2009-12-31, 2010-03-31 (the series has June 2009, then quarterly data from June 2010). They are absent, not filled.
- SBP debt-to-GDP ratios and GDP: 18 fiscal-year ends (2009-06-30 to 2026-06-30). SBP publishes no quarterly ratio: the other 49 quarters are blank in EasyData and are not computed here (annual GDP cannot be used for a quarter).
- Ministry of Finance Annual Debt Review FY-26: 3 fiscal-year ends (2024-06-30, 2025-06-30, 2026-06-30).

## 3. Accounting identities (SBP)

- Gross public debt = gross government domestic debt + government external debt + debt from the IMF: holds in 67 of 67 quarters (tolerance 0.15 billion: older quarters are stored to one decimal, so a sum of three rounded values can differ by up to 0.15).
- Total debt of the government (FRDLA) = gross public debt - government deposits with the banking system: holds in 67 of 67 quarters.
- Each published ratio = the stock / GDP x 100: 90 published ratios checked, all within 0.06 percentage points.

## 6. Not included

- Government-guaranteed debt of public sector enterprises is in SBP's series (PSE domestic and external debt) but is not government debt and is not shown as such; it is listed only within "total debt and liabilities".
- Debt servicing and interest payments: the MoF review reports external debt servicing in US dollars for three years only, and federal interest payments sit in the fiscal-operations tables (a different dataset). Not included in this release.
- Monthly composition of domestic debt by instrument (SBP dataset TS_GP_BAM_GOVTDDL_M, 50 series) exists and is a candidate for a later release.
- Before June 2009: not in this SBP dataset.
