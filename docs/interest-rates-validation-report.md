## 3. Units and scale

- Every EasyData series used declares its unit as "Percent" (checked per series), and every value lies inside the plausible 0-40% range (the build stops otherwise).
- SBP's auction files print percent per annum to four decimals; the 1997-2022 lending/deposit sheets print percent to two decimals; the Jul-2022 sheet prints full precision (kept as printed).
- **OMO unit conversion.** All 1,871 OMO cut-off rates are stored by SBP as fractions (0 < value < 1; the build stops if one is not). They are multiplied by 100. Independent check: in all 707 operations where SBP also printed the range of bid quotes (in percent), the converted cut-off lies inside that range (0 exceptions; the build stops on any).
- OMO cut-offs against the SBP corridor in force on the day (from 17 Aug 2009): 1705 of 1722 inside the floor-ceiling band; 17 outside (2013-06-07 injection 9.52%; 2013-06-14 injection 9.58%; 2013-06-21 injection 9.58%; 2014-01-31 injection 10.05%; 2014-02-07 injection 10.08%; 2014-03-28 injection 10.1%; 2014-04-01 injection 10.11%; 2014-04-04 injection 10.15%; …).
- Overnight repo rate against the corridor: 2795 of 2797 days inside; 2 outside (2015-06-30 7.02%; 2020-04-16 11.01%). A market rate can trade outside an administered band; these days are shown as observed.
